# 菜品介绍

## claypot
煲仔飯的起源可以追溯到中國南方的農村地區。早在明清時期，廣東、福建等地的農民在勞動之餘，會將米、菜、肉等食材放入瓦煲中，用慢火燉煮，製作出美味的煲仔飯。這種烹調方式因簡單、便利、營養豐富而深受歡迎，逐漸流傳至香港。

The origin of claypot rice can be traced back to the rural areas of southern China. As early as the Ming and Qing dynasties, farmers in Guangdong, Fujian and other regions would put rice, vegetables, meat and other ingredients into clay pots and simmer them over slow fire to make delicious claypot rice. This cooking method became deeply popular due to its simplicity, convenience and nutritional value, and gradually spread to Hong Kong.


康哥是香港嚐囍煲仔小菜的主廚，對煲仔飯有著深厚的經驗和見解。我們通過電話聯繫與親自拜訪，誠摯邀請他參與採訪，他欣然應允並分享了寶貴的烹飪心得。
Kang is the head chef at Chang Hei Claypot Rice in Hong Kong, with deep expertise and insights into claypot rice. Through phone calls and in-person visits, we sincerely invited him to participate in the interview. He graciously accepted and shared his valuable culinary insights.


## hakka-tofu
客家釀豆腐作為一道極具代表性的客家名菜，承載著深厚的歷史文化內涵，其起源與發展和客家人的遷徙歷程緊密相連。

身為客家人，小李（化名）為我們展示了他們家庭製作客家釀豆腐的過程，並嘗試使用AI記錄了不同的偶然性，及根據AI的提示，創作了一道保留傳統味道的創新菜。

As a representative Hakka dish, Hakka Stuffed Tofu carries profound historical and cultural connotations. Its origin and development are closely linked to the migration of Hakka people.

## steam-buns
包子是一道歷史悠久的中國傳統美食，深受人們喜愛。最早可追溯至古代，因其方便攜帶且營養豐富，曾是勞工和旅人常食之物。隨著時間的推移，包子逐漸成為各地飲食文化中的重要組成部分，每個地區都發展出了獨特的餡料和風味。

Steam buns are a traditional Chinese delicacy with a long history, deeply loved by people. Dating back to ancient times, they were once common food for laborers and travelers due to their portability and nutritional value. Over time, steam buns have become an important part of regional food cultures across China, with each area developing unique fillings and flavors.

在英國，「包子」常被誤認為是台灣的荷葉包，一種夾著滷肉的折疊式蒸包。為了打破這個刻板印象，並向大眾推廣真正的中式包子，廚師包創立了包公包子品牌。

In the UK, "steam buns" are often mistaken for Taiwanese lotus leaf buns, a folded steamed bun filled with braised meat. To break this stereotype and promote authentic Chinese steam buns to the public, Chef Bao founded the Bao Gong steam bun brand.

我們很榮幸邀請到廚師王，與我們分享她對包子的見解，探討這一傳統美食的深厚文化底蘊及其在現代社會的演變。

We are honored to have invited Chef Wang to share her insights on steam buns, exploring the profound cultural heritage of this traditional delicacy and its evolution in modern society.

## liuyang-steamed-ribs
瀏陽蒸排骨是湖南瀏陽地區的一道經典家常菜，以蒸製手法保留了食材的原汁原味，搭配豆豉的鹹香與辣椒的微辣，形成了獨特的地方風味。這道菜的精髓在於「蒸」——不用大火爆炒，而是讓時間與蒸汽慢慢將味道滲入每一塊骨肉之中。

Liuyang steamed ribs is a classic home-style dish from the Liuyang region of Hunan. Using steaming techniques to preserve the original flavors of ingredients, combined with the savory aroma of fermented black beans and mild heat of chili peppers, it creates a unique local taste. The essence of this dish lies in "steaming" - instead of stir-frying over high heat, it allows time and steam to slowly infuse flavors into every piece of meat and bone.

我們邀請到了湘菜主廚張師傅，分享了他對瀏陽蒸排骨的理解，以及如何在保持傳統工藝的同時，用AI探索食材搭配的新可能。

We invited Chef Zhang, a Xiang cuisine master chef, to share his understanding of Liuyang steamed ribs and how to use AI to explore new possibilities in ingredient pairing while maintaining traditional craftsmanship.

## stir-fried-beef
小炒黃牛牛肉是湖南湘菜中的代表性菜品之一，以鮮嫩的黃牛肉搭配辣椒、蒜薹等佐料，用大火快炒而成，講究「鍋氣」——那種只有猛火與鐵鍋碰撞才能產生的獨特香氣。牛肉嫩滑、辣味鮮明、口感豐富，是湘菜館裡點單率最高的硬菜之一。

Stir-fried beef is one of the representative dishes of Hunan Xiang cuisine. Featuring tender yellow beef paired with chili peppers, garlic sprouts, and other seasonings, it's quickly stir-fried over high heat, emphasizing "wok hei" - the unique aroma that can only be produced by the collision of fierce fire and iron wok. The beef is tender and smooth, with distinct spicy flavors and rich texture, making it one of the most popular main dishes in Xiang cuisine restaurants.

我們邀請到了湘菜廚師劉師傅，分享了他對小炒黃牛肉火候掌控的心得，並嘗試用AI模擬不同炒制參數下的風味變化。

We invited Chef Liu, a Xiang cuisine chef, to share his insights on controlling the heat for stir-fried beef, and attempted to use AI to simulate flavor changes under different stir-frying parameters.

## preserved-veg-pork
梅菜豬肉又稱梅菜扣肉，是一道源自客家地區的經典名菜。精選五花豬肉經過煮、炸、蒸多道工序，肥而不膩、入口即化；搭配醃製已久的梅菜，其特有的鹹香與甘甜完美中和了肉的油潤，形成層次豐富的口感。這道菜承載著客家人勤儉持家的智慧——用醃製保存食材的方法，將平凡材料昇華為宴席佳餚。

Preserved vegetable pork, also known as Meicai Kou Rou, is a classic dish originating from the Hakka region. Selected pork belly undergoes multiple processes of boiling, frying, and steaming, resulting in meat that is rich but not greasy, melting in the mouth. Paired with long-preserved preserved vegetables, their unique savory and sweet flavors perfectly balance the richness of the meat, creating a multi-layered texture. This dish embodies the Hakka wisdom of thrift and household management - using preservation methods to elevate ordinary ingredients into banquet delicacies.

我們邀請到了客家料理傳承人陳阿姨，展示了她家傳的梅菜豬肉製作技法，並探索AI如何幫助記錄和傳承這門手藝。

We invited Auntie Chen, a Hakka cuisine inheritor, to demonstrate her family's traditional techniques for making preserved vegetable pork, and explored how AI can help record and pass on this culinary art.

## vermicelli-shrimp
粉絲蝦煲是粵式海鮮家常菜中的溫暖代表。吸滿蝦汁與高湯的粉絲滑嫩入味，彈牙的鮮蝦帶來海洋的清甜，金针菇與蔬菜增添了豐富的口感層次。整道菜在砂鍋中燜煮至滋滋作響，端上桌時仍熱氣騰騰，是家人團聚時最溫馨的滋味。

Vermicelli shrimp pot is a heartwarming representative of Cantonese home-style seafood dishes. The vermicelli, soaked in shrimp juice and broth, is smooth and flavorful; the springy fresh shrimp brings the refreshing sweetness of the ocean; enoki mushrooms and vegetables add rich texture layers. The entire dish is braised in a clay pot until sizzling, served steaming hot at the table, making it the most heartwarming taste for family gatherings.

我們邀請到了粵菜廚師何師傅，分享了他對粉絲蝦煲的烹飪秘訣，以及如何運用AI構思海鮮與粉絲的新穎搭配方式。

We invited Chef He, a Cantonese chef, to share his cooking secrets for vermicelli shrimp pot, and how to use AI to conceptualize novel pairing methods for seafood and vermicelli.
