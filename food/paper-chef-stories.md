## 1. claypot — 煲仔饭（P2 / 粤菜主厨，30 年经验）

### 背景
P2 是一位拥有 30 年粤菜经验的专业厨师。他参与了煲仔饭的 GenAI 共创实验，并在过程中表达了对煲仔饭技艺与传承的深入思考。

### 论文 A：创新尝试
> "P2 attempted to generate variations of Claypot Rice suited to different regional tastes, exploring possibilities to attract more people to enjoy this dish (Fig.2 B) and C))."
>
> （AI 生成了"泰式椰子鸡煲仔饭"和"水果风味煲仔饭"两个融合版本。）

### 论文 B：关于"锅"的故事
> "P2, for instance, pointed out the influence of different rice pots used over the years to cook Claypot Rice. The cooking pots have evolved, and with these changes, the texture and flavor of the rice have been affected. P2 generated images of rice pots from different eras, visually highlighting the impact that the choice of cooking vessel can have on the dish."

### 论文 B：用 AI 教学徒（原话）
> "P2 pointed out the usefulness of AI in visualizing these errors. He suggested that when teaching apprentices, AI could be employed to show the difference in outcomes based on varying heat levels, which would help them understand the effects of their mistakes."

### 论文 B：P2 用手势表达技艺
> "P2 also has similar expressions. He uses rich gestures as shown in the picture to express the use of ingredients."

### 论文 B：P2 对"米饭蓬松度"的判断（原话）
当 AI 生成的煲仔饭图像中米饭过于压实、不够松散时，他亲自给 AI 校正了 prompt：

> P2: **"The rice was pressed with a spoon, but it was pressed too much, so it is not as loose as before, and the quality of the rice is not as good as before. It's true. The air enters, but the heat is not enough, so it is forced out. The rice is not strong enough to withstand the heat, so it explodes when it is heated, and it will steam when there is enough steam."**

### 论文 B：煲仔饭"煨"的状态难以言传（原话）
> P2: **"At the beginning, the rice needs time to cook; it requires time to roast. It's not a matter of cooking it and having it done immediately. The process seems to destroy the ingredients; it's too intense and damages them. It's a slow simmer. At one point, when the charcoal isn't burning, you have to add more charcoal."**

> 论文补充说明：P2 "faced challenges when describing the dimensions and preparation method of Chinese sausages. He found it difficult to articulate the precise size and technique using words alone; instead, he resorted to hand gestures to convey how the sausage should be sliced."

## 2. hakka-tofu — 客家酿豆腐（P1 / 客家菜业余厨师，10 年经验）

### 背景
P1 是有 10 年客家菜烹饪经验的业余厨师，本身是客家人，使用 GenAI 之前已有几个月的 ChatGPT/中文 LLM 使用经验。

### 论文 A：寻求"简化版"传家菜
> "P1 inquired about a simpler way to prepare Hakka-style Stuffed Tofu, aiming to create an easy version for home cooks who love the dish."

### 论文 A：AI 加入蔬菜的健康版
> "An innovative version of Hakka-style Stuffed Tofu generated by GenAI included tomatoes and beans as side garnishes. P1 recognized this imagination, noting that the addition of vegetables not only enhanced the visual appeal of the dish but also increased its nutritional value."

### 论文 A：拒绝完全照搬 AI 食谱（原话）
AI 提出了一种叫 "Garden-Styled Stuffed Tofu"（园林风格酿豆腐）的创新版，P1 欣赏其摆盘但不接受其食材：

> "GenAI provided P1 with an innovative version of Stuffed Tofu—'Garden-Styled Stuffed Tofu.' Although he appreciated the visual appeal, he pointed out that **the tomato used was not a variety commonly found in Chinese cuisine** (Fig. 6 B)."

最终他只采纳了摆盘建议，自己重制时仅加入玉米：

> P1: **"Following that recipe, the fillings won't be the taste of Hakka Stuffed Tofu. The only adjustment I made was adding some corn to the stuffing."**

> "He also noted that he was able to accept the plating suggestion because it did not alter the flavor of the main dish."

### 论文 B：海外烹饪缺料的痛点
> "P1 also supported this observation, mentioning that when making Hakka-style Stuffed Tofu, the absence of starch for thickening the sauce would result in a less tender and smooth texture."

### 论文 B：AI 无法传递情感（原话）
> P1: **"AI cannot present the things we find truly delicious. It's hard to explain; there's an emotional difference that AI just doesn't convey."**

## 3. steam-buns — 包子 / 灌汤包（P6 / 男 27 岁，点心主厨 7 年）

### 背景
P6 是有 7 年点心经验的专业厨师，准备的是"灌汤包"（Juicy Soup Buns）。他有设计背景，对图像的细节非常敏感。

### 论文 B：揉面手势难表达（原话）
> P6: **"I don't even know how to describe the hand gesture for shaping buns, and even if I did, GPT probably wouldn't understand. I think AI still has limitations when it comes to complex handmade techniques like folding pleats. It might be better to simplify it to just holding a bun in hand."**

### 论文 B：法式糕点店学来的"poolish"前晚发酵（原话，重要的传承故事）
> P6: **"You're missing a step in this dough fermentation process—I usually prepare a 'poolish' starter the night before. The poolish is made by mixing 100g of water, 100g of flour, and 1g of dry yeast, then leaving it in the fridge overnight. When kneading the dough the next day, I add the poolish in. This helps delay the aging of the dough and makes the steamed buns softer. I learned this method while working at a French pastry shop, combining Western and Chinese techniques."**

### 论文 B：AI 生成的"和面钩"是西式的（原话）
> P6: **"The dough hook generated here is incorrect; it generated a Western-style croissant dough hook. This type of hook makes the dough softer, but for making buns, we use this spiral-shaped dough hook."**

> "Its pasta pressing machine is also Western style, it is the kind used for French cuisine."

### 论文 B：AI 图像太"假"（设计师视角，原话）
> P6: **"Since I studied design, I don't like this overly greasy visual style—it's obvious that it's AI-generated. Look, the chopsticks in these images are all at the exact same 45-degree angle, and the folds on the buns look too fake."** (Fig.6)

### 论文 B：面团暴露问题
> "P6 explained that when preparing dough, if it is not covered properly, it will dry out and crack, affecting the texture of the dish."

## 4. liuyang-steamed-ribs — 浏阳蒸排骨（P7 / 女 25 岁，江西菜业余厨师 6 年）

### 背景
P7 是有 6 年江西菜经验的业余厨师，参与前已有 ChatGPT 使用经验。她是工作坊中对 AI 期待较高、要求也较严苛的参与者之一。

### 论文 A：探索套餐式健康版
> "P7 considered creating a multi-dish set meal inspired by their expertise in Liuyang Steamed Cuisine (Fig.4 B)). GenAI suggested that, in addition to Liuyang Steamed Pork Ribs as the main dish, complementary dishes like steamed fish with scallion sauce and steamed tofu could be included as high-protein additions."

### 论文 A：分子料理版的失败（原话）
> "P7 explored a molecular gastronomy version of Liuyang Steamed Pork Ribs using GenAI (Fig.4 C)). However, the AI's suggested sauce (which included lemon juice and honey) **strayed too far from the authentic salty, spicy, and savory flavors of the dish**. P7 felt that GenAI's understanding of preserving original tastes was insufficient, and she was disappointed that she could not incorporate its suggestion."

### 论文 A：要求换用国产 LLM（原话）
> P7 spontaneously requested to use Doubao instead of ChatGPT after a few trials. She said: **"I would like to try Doubao for better image generation for Chinese cuisine. I believe it's better."**

### 论文 B：用干辣椒粉替代鲜辣椒（原话）
> "P7, for example, described an interesting variation of Liuyang Steamed Pork Ribs by using dried chili powder instead of the usual fresh chili peppers. This simple swap resulted in a completely different flavor profile, where the heat from the dried chili added a smoky and earthy depth to the dish."
>
> P7: **"When I steam the ribs, replacing the usual ingredients with dried chili powder gives it a completely different flavor."**

### 论文 B：柴火灶与木蒸笼带来的独特香气
> "P7, on the other hand, described how the use of a wood-fired stove and wooden steaming baskets for steaming pork ribs can result in a significantly enhanced flavor. According to P7, **the aroma from burning wood infuses the food during the cooking process, creating a unique fragrance that cannot be replicated by modern gas stoves or electric steamers**."

## 5. stir-fried-beef — 湘式小炒黄牛肉（P4 / 男 50 岁，伦敦湘菜馆主厨 30 年）

### 背景
P4 是有 30 年湖南菜经验的专业厨师，目前在伦敦的一家餐厅工作。论文中他多次强调因海外原料受限而做出的妥协。

### 论文 B：缺辣椒，用土耳其辣椒替代（原话）
> "P4, a chef with 30 years of experience in Hunan cuisine working at a restaurant in London, shared that the restaurant struggles to find the same variety of chili peppers commonly used in China. To address this, they substitute the Chinese spiral chili peppers with Turkish chili peppers to maintain freshness and reduce costs."

### 论文 B：为外国客人调整切肉与辣度
> "P4 explained that since the restaurant serves many foreign customers, dishes like black pepper beef often need to be adapted to suit local tastes. To standardize the presentation and ensure consistency in the dishes, **he cuts all beef slices to the same thickness, even for dishes like Hunan Style Stir-fried Beef, which originally uses thin, square beef slices in the traditional Hunan style. When preparing this dish for foreign customers, P4 reduces the amount of chili to better accommodate their palates**."

### 论文 B：火候经验（炒牛肉）
> "P4 also noted that when the heat is too high while stir-frying beef, the meat becomes tough and the bottom of the pan can burn."

### 论文 B：批评 AI 没把辣椒画对（原话）
> P4: **"The peppers in the generated image are not in the right state. They should be chopped, not whole peppers."** (Fig.3)
>
> P4: **"The peppers look like they haven't been cooked, they still appear very fresh. It feels more like different ingredients have just been assembled together."**

> "For example, P4 thought that the size of the peppers generated by AI was not right."

## 6. preserved-veg-pork — 梅菜豬肉 / 梅菜扣肉（P9 / 女 27 岁，江浙菜业余 6 年，伦敦缅甸餐厅工作）

### 背景
P9 是有 6 年江浙菜经验的业余厨师，目前在伦敦一家缅甸餐厅工作。她传承了母亲的家传烹饪方法，是"家族传承"主题的代表参与者。

### 论文 B：海外猪肉切得太薄（原话）
> "P9, a chef working at a Burmese restaurant, noted that the pork belly available abroad is typically cut into thinner strips, unlike the thicker cuts used in China, leading to smaller pieces of pork belly being used. This, in turn, changes the texture and flavor of the dish."

### 论文 B：制作梅菜扣肉的关键细节
> "P9 mentioned that when making Stewed Pork with Preserved Vegetables, **failing to create a well in the ingredients for the braising sauce will result in uneven seasoning, leading to a less flavorful dish**."

### 论文 B：糖与香料的小窍门
> "P9 mentioned that she usually uses brown sugar instead of rock sugar when making caramel, as rock sugar tends to burn easily."
>
> "P9 further emphasized that garlic should be added just before the dish is finished cooking to release its full fragrance."

### 论文 B：母亲传授的猪油炒梅菜（原话，家族传承故事）
> "P9 reflected on their mother's unique cooking method for frying dried mustard greens with lard, a technique passed down through family tradition. However, they noted the challenge of replicating this practice in London due to the unavailability of certain ingredients like lard, which led to subtle adaptations in their cooking."
>
> P9: **"This method is still a bit different from how it's done back home because this dish was taught to me by my mother. When she stir-fried dried mustard greens, she would add some lard, but in London, I don't have the conditions to do that."**

### 论文 B：缅甸餐厅的香叶处理方式
> "P9 also recounted memories from their experience working at a Burmese restaurant, where dried bay leaves were roasted on an iron griddle in a method distinct from Chinese practices."
>
> "P9 further noted that omitting the aromatic leaves typically grilled on a hot iron plate in their restaurant would negatively affect the dish's flavor."

## 7. vermicelli-shrimp — 蒜蓉粉絲蝦煲（P5 / 女 30 岁，川渝菜业余 10 年，海外 15 年）

### 背景
P5 是有 10 年川渝菜经验的业余厨师，已在海外居住 15 年。她对食材短缺与口味本地化有深切体会。

### 论文 B：找不到带头大虾（原话）
> "P5, a food amateur who has lived abroad for 15 years, explained that **typical foreign supermarkets usually don't carry large prawns with heads. As a result, when she uses the more commonly available peeled shrimp, the flavor is not as robust as when using whole prawns**."

### 论文 B：粉丝泡发的细节
> "P5 also noted that when preparing Garlic Vermicelli Shrimp Pot, **if the vermicelli is soaked for too long, it becomes too soft, and the temperature of the water used to soak the vermicelli can also impact the texture**."

### 论文 B：为外国朋友改良口味（原话）
> "P5 also shared her experience of adjusting recipes when cooking for foreign friends, such as making Garlic Vermicelli Shrimp Pot by **replacing cilantro with parsley, using butter instead of soy sauce for the garlic sauce, and adding onions for extra flavor**."

### 论文 B：蒜末的"焯水技巧"（原话）
> P5: **"I thought of a small cooking tip: blanch the minced garlic first; otherwise, it's likely to burn and turn too dark when frying."**

> "P5 explained that adding garlic to the oil before it has reached the correct temperature results in less aromatic garlic."

### 论文 B：对 AI 表现失败菜的怀疑
> "P5 expressed skepticism about AI's ability to accurately represent cooking errors, noting that humans typically avoid photographing failed dishes, and AI often lacks the data to illustrate common mistakes, such as overly wet dough or failed fermentation in bun-making."

### 论文 B：AI 把虾画得太卡通（原话）
> P5: **"The style of the shrimp is a bit too cartoonish; it looks somewhat fake."** (Fig.5)

## 附：跨参与者共性观察（来自论文 B 总结）

- **海外食材限制**：P4（湖南）、P5（粤川）、P9（江浙）、P1（客家）四位身在海外的师傅一致强调，**"the inability to find specific ingredients abroad significantly affects the flavor of the dishes they prepare"**。
- **手势 vs. 文字**：P2 与 P6 都提到，许多关键技艺只能用手势演示而无法用文字表达，AI 因此难以记录复杂的手工技法。
- **情感与经验的不可生成性**：P1 说 "AI cannot present the things we find truly delicious. It's hard to explain; there's an emotional difference that AI just doesn't convey."
