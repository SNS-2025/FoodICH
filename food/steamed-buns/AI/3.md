這張發麵的步驟缺少了一個重要步驟，那就是我會提前一晚製作波蘭種。波蘭種是用 100g 水、100g 麵粉和 1g 乾酵母混合，然後放入冰箱發酵一夜。之後和麵時，可以把它加入麵團，這樣能延緩麵團老化，使蒸出來的包子更加鬆軟。因為我以前在法式甜點店打工，這是我偷偷學來的方法，算是中西融合一下


You’re missing a step in this dough fermentation process.I usually prepare a "poolish" starter the night before. The poolish is made by mixing 100g of water, 100g of flour, and 1g of dry yeast, then leaving it in the fridge overnight. When kneading the dough the next day, I add the poolish in. This helps delay the aging of the dough and makes the steamed buns softer. I learned this method while working at a French pastry shop, combining Western and Chinese techniques.
