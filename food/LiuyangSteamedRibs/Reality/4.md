接著加入適量的蒜末、辣椒、鹽和豆豉，豆豉是瀏陽蒸菜的靈魂調料，為排骨帶來獨特的發酵香氣和鹹鮮風味，是這道菜不可替代的精髓。

Next, add appropriate amounts of minced garlic, chili, salt, and fermented black beans. The fermented black beans are the soul seasoning of Liuyang steamed dishes, bringing a unique fermented aroma and savory flavor to the ribs—this is the irreplaceable essence of the dish.
