師傅會轉動瓦煲來觀察火力變化，當看見白煙冒出時，便能判斷火候是否得當。透過這種方式精準掌控火力，才能確保米飯煮熟後香氣四溢，並在煲底形成金黃酥脆的飯焦。

The chef rotates the clay pot to observe the heat distribution. When white smoke begins to rise, it indicates the intensity of the flame. By monitoring the smoke and adjusting accordingly, the chef ensures the rice cooks evenly with a fragrant aroma and develops a golden, crispy crust at the bottom.
