若米飯煮製時間過長，我們會將其改製成白飯，用於炒飯等料理。因為米飯一旦過了「氣」，食材便無法再吸收飯香，繼續煲製便失去了意義。

If the rice has been cooked for too long, we will set it aside as plain steamed rice, suitable for making fried rice and other dishes. Once the rice has lost its "qi" (vital essence), the ingredients can no longer absorb its aroma, making further clay pot cooking unnecessary.
