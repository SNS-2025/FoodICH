將浸泡後的米粒倒入漏勺中，充分瀝乾多餘水分。接著將米放入鍋中短暫煮製，待米粒半熟後，隨即轉移至瓦煲中，置於火爐上繼續烹煮，使米飯在瓦煲內充分吸收熱力，形成鍋巴。

Pour the soaked rice into a colander and drain thoroughly to remove excess water. Then briefly parboil the rice in a pot until partially cooked, before promptly transferring it into a clay pot set over a stove. Continue cooking in the clay pot to allow the rice to fully absorb the heat and form a crispy golden crust at the bottom.
