調製勾芡汁：將生抽、蠔油、鹽、食用油與玉米粉混合，加入少許蒜末增香。將勾芡汁倒入鍋中，輕輕翻炒使醬汁濃稠光亮，均勻裹在豆腐表面。

Prepare the thickening sauce by mixing light soy sauce, oyster sauce, salt, cooking oil, and cornstarch, adding a touch of minced garlic for aroma. Pour the sauce into the pan and gently toss, allowing it to thicken into a glossy glaze that coats each piece of tofu evenly.
