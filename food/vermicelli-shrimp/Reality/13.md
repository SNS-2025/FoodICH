打開鍋蓋，將事先炒好的蒜蓉醬均勻澆在蝦和粉絲上，最後撒上翠綠的蔥花和紅艷的小米辣作為點綴。一道色香味俱全的粉絲蝦煲便大功告成！

Open the lid and pour the prepared garlic sauce evenly over the shrimp and vermicelli. Finally, sprinkle with bright green chopped scallions and vibrant red chili peppers as garnish. A perfectly colorful and aromatic vermicelli shrimp pot is now complete!
