鍋中倒入較多的食用油，大火燒熱後轉中火，放入蒜末慢慢爆香。注意火候，蒜末呈金黃色時香氣最佳，過火則會變苦。

Pour a generous amount of cooking oil into the wok. Heat over high flame, then reduce to medium heat and add the minced garlic, slowly frying until fragrant. Watch the heat carefully—garlic reaches its peak aroma when golden brown, but overcooking will make it bitter.
