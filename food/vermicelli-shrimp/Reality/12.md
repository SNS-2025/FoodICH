蓋上砂鍋蓋子，以中火煲煮約30分鐘。期間蝦的鮮味、菌菇的香氣與粉絲完美融合，形成一鍋鮮香四溢的美味佳餚。

Cover the clay pot with its lid and cook over medium heat for about 30 minutes. During this time, the shrimp's sweetness, the mushrooms' aroma, and the vermicelli meld together perfectly, creating a pot of fragrant, mouthwatering deliciousness.
