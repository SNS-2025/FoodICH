取適量優質梅乾菜，提前浸泡清洗，去除多餘鹽分和雜質，擠乾水分後備用。梅乾菜的獨特風味是這道菜的靈魂所在。

Prepare an appropriate amount of high-quality preserved mustard greens by soaking and rinsing them beforehand to remove excess salt and impurities. Squeeze out the water and set aside—the distinctive flavor of these preserved greens is the soul of this dish.
